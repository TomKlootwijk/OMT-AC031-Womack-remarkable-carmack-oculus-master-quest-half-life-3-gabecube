8.2 A preserved non-monotonicity
Take two valid bits and choose
𝑥 = 112, 𝑀 ASA = 𝑀NA = 𝑉 = 112, 𝑀 𝜕 = 102, 𝑀 fringe = 012. (13)

9 Precedence zero and the integrated record
9.1 Ordering, not an elapsed-time value
C calls the blend “precedence order 0” and associates it with immediate hardware action. This
revision retainsprecedence 0as the name of the source’s pinion/blend interpretation stage. Its
formal consequence is a dependency order: required source values and their encoding must be
available before the corresponding Boolean value can be defined. [C, pp.1–3][I07]
The source gives no hardware scheduler, memory-ordering rule, latency bound or proof of a
zero-duration execution. The normative order here therefore stateswhat depends on what, without
inserting an elapsed-time guarantee into the Boolean algebra.
