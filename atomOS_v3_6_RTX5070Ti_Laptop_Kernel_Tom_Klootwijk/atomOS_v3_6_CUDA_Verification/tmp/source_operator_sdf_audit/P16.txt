deflected, or tightly curved paths governed by electromotive forces, particle mass, 
and collisions with air molecules. [1, 2]

at relativistic or near-relativistic speeds, the downward acceleration of Earth's gravity 
has a completely negligible effect on their path over the few centimeters they travel 
inside a chamber. [1, 2]

of flight. [1, 2]
