\section{A finite exact field kernel with work and heat balance}
\label{sec:r14-field-step}
The common continuous energy law needs a separately derived finite update.
This section supplies one for a fixed quadratic electromagnetic/mechanical
reduction with one thermal store. It extends the linear mechanical midpoint
profile to a coupled passive finite field. Temperature-dependent coefficients,
nonlinear energy and moving bases retain the continuous model and need their
own selected update; they are not covered by this fixed-coefficient proof.

\subsection{FIELD-MIDPOINT-QUADRATIC-R1}
Let $x$ contain the retained field and mechanical coordinates after resolving
algebraic constraints. Declare constant matrices
\begin{equation}
 \mathsf Q=\mathsf Q^T\succ0,\qquad
 \mathsf J^T=-\mathsf J,\qquad
 \mathsf R=\mathsf R^T\succeq0,\qquad
 \mathsf A=(\mathsf J-\mathsf R)\mathsf Q,
\end{equation}
and a constant input map $\mathsf B$. The model is
\begin{equation}
 \dot x=\mathsf A x+\mathsf B u,\qquad
 E_f(x)=\tfrac12 x^T\mathsf Qx,\qquad
 y=\mathsf B^T\mathsf Qx.
 \label{eq:r14-field-linear}
\end{equation}
Each coordinate keeps its own dimensions. In particular
$[\mathsf Q_{ij}]=\mathrm{J}/([x_i][x_j])$, the input/output pairing
$u^Ty$ has power units, and $\mathsf A_{ij}$ has units
$[x_i]/([x_j]\,\mathrm{s})$. Positive definiteness refers to the energy form
on independent coordinates; it cannot be asserted before removing gauge or
other null directions. Fixed constrained Maxwell--Lorentz modes and a fixed
positive mechanical stiffness are examples when their energy has this form.

For $h\ge0$, held discrete input $\bar u$ and
$\bar x=(x^++x)/2$, define the undivided midpoint equation
\begin{equation}
 x^+-x=h(\mathsf A\bar x+\mathsf B\bar u).
 \label{eq:r14-field-midpoint}
\end{equation}
Its complete finite body is
\begin{align}
 \mathsf D_h&=I-\tfrac h2\mathsf A,\nonumber\\
 b_h&=(I+\tfrac h2\mathsf A)x+h\mathsf B\bar u,\nonumber\\
 x^+&=\operatorname{SOLVE\_LINEAR}(\mathsf D_h,b_h),
 \qquad \bar x=\tfrac12(x^++x).
 \label{eq:r14-field-solve}
\end{align}
For $h=0$ this gives $x^+=x$ without a division by time.

\begin{proposition}[Unique finite solve]
Under the stated hypotheses, $\mathsf D_h$ is invertible for every $h\ge0$.
If the matrix entries, state, step and input are rational, the output is rational.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
If $\mathsf D_hw=0$, then $w=(h/2)(\mathsf J-\mathsf R)\mathsf Qw$.
Multiplication by $w^T\mathsf Q$ gives
\[
 w^T\mathsf Qw=-\tfrac h2(\mathsf Qw)^T\mathsf R(\mathsf Qw)\le0.
\]
Positive definiteness forces $w=0$, so the square matrix is nonsingular.
A finite nonsingular rational linear system has a rational solution.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The same update supplies its exact energy ledger}
Define the interval work and deposited dissipative energy
\begin{equation}
 W=h\bar u^T\mathsf B^T\mathsf Q\bar x,\qquad
 D=h(\mathsf Q\bar x)^T\mathsf R(\mathsf Q\bar x)\ge0.
 \label{eq:r14-field-ledger}
\end{equation}
Symmetry and the midpoint equation give
\begin{align}
 E_f(x^+)-E_f(x)
 &=\bar x^T\mathsf Q(x^+-x)\nonumber\\
 &=h(\mathsf Q\bar x)^T(\mathsf J-\mathsf R)\mathsf Q\bar x
   +h\bar x^T\mathsf Q\mathsf B\bar u=W-D.
 \label{eq:r14-field-energy}
\end{align}
Thus a lossless unforced instance preserves the selected quadratic field
energy, and a passive unforced instance cannot increase it. The skew term
exchanges energy between retained coordinates without creating it.

Here $\mathsf R$ represents only losses thermalized in the selected store.
Outgoing radiation or power delivered to an external load belongs in boundary
ports or its own store; it is not automatically deposited as local heat.
Connect all of this profile's dissipation to one thermal store of fixed heat
capacity $C_\theta>0$, with $T>0$, no external heat flow and
\begin{equation}
 E_\theta=C_\theta T,\qquad
 S_\theta=C_\theta\log(T/T_\star),\quad T_\star>0,\qquad
 T^+=T+D/C_\theta.
 \label{eq:r14-field-thermal}
\end{equation}
The arbitrary energy reference is omitted. The temperature remains positive,
and direct substitution proves
\begin{align}
 \Delta(E_f+E_\theta)&=W,\nonumber\\
 \Delta S_\theta&=C_\theta\log\left(1+\frac{D}{C_\theta T}\right)\ge0.
 \label{eq:r14-field-total}
\end{align}
This is a complete finite field/heat update. It deposits the energy $D$ once;
adding the same loss again as an independent heater would violate the identity.
Heat leaving the selected thermal store requires an additional explicit
exchange model. This one-store closure deliberately has no feedback from its
temperature into $\mathsf Q,\mathsf R$ during the interval.

\subsection{Constraints and representation}
For a constant linear constraint map $\mathsf L$ such that
$\mathsf L\mathsf A=0$ and
$\mathsf L\mathsf B\bar u=0$, multiplication of
\eqref{eq:r14-field-midpoint} yields
$\mathsf Lx^+=\mathsf Lx$. This is a conditional preservation result;
an arbitrary modal discretization need not satisfy those identities. For
charge-driven Maxwell fields, changing charge and currents need a compatible
continuity state and constraint law, rather than a falsely constant constraint.

The template exports $(x^+,T^+,W,D)$ and reads one old snapshot. Its body uses
finite scalar operations, typed matrix contraction and the existing
\code{SOLVE\_LINEAR} operator. Rational $C_\theta,T$ also give a rational
next temperature; the logarithm may remain exact syntax if entropy is exported.
The packing transform then preserves these operators and operands literally.
No PDE opcode, arbitrary-sign oracle or new executable backend is required by
the mathematical definition.

The exact residual
\[
 r_{\rm total}=E_f(x^+)+C_\theta T^+-E_f(x)-C_\theta T-W
\]
vanishes by the displayed proof. These are discrete work and heat quantities,
not a claim that they equal the integrals along the continuous trajectory.
Conservation does not establish trajectory accuracy at an arbitrary step size.
Changing a coefficient first requires its parameter-work ledger; nonlinear
coupling cannot be added to this step while retaining the fixed quadratic proof.
