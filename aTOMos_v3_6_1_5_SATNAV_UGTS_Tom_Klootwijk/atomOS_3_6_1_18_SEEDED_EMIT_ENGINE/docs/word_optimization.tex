\section{Measured exact word-kernel optimization}\label{sec:word-optimization}

Across 17 equal working sizes, the optimized implementation beat baseline in 59 of 68 GPU-median comparisons and 54 of 68 host-wall-median comparisons. GPU baseline/optimized ratios range from 0.8048 to 1.254.

This is the bounded integer phi-expression, ASA/NA+JK and digest workload, \emph{not an S2 comparison}. Both load paths receive direct streaming addresses, exact bank shift/mask indexing, integer reciprocal remainder with one correction, proved-safe 32-bit coefficient arithmetic and warp digest reductions. Seeds, recurrence, masks, reset/warm policy and independent 64-bit CPU oracle are unchanged.

Pairs require equal working bytes, seed, epoch/trial/warm counts, chunk size and bank layout. Every corresponding trial's address parameters and every epoch's XOR, modular-sum and visit-count digest match, including the final epoch. Digest collisions remain possible; large sets retain sampled CPU replay. All raw trials and unmatched sizes are retained in \path{review/word_optimization_summary.json}.

Optimized maximum: 10462691328 working bytes (9.744 GiB), 277.2 times the reported 36 MiB L2, 81.61\% of reported device memory. It covers 326959104 records over 20 banks per epoch; allocation requests total 10471136648 bytes and 1084227584 bytes were reported free after allocation.

Working bytes include 16N immutable bytes and both 8N-byte state buffers. Every record is visited each epoch. The maxima are independent observations; they are paired only when their actual sizes match. This extent is not active-warp occupancy, measured cache residency or zero-headroom allocation. Shared-device free-memory changes are not exclusive process peak measurements.

\subsection{Scope and reproducibility}

Baseline and optimized runs were separate; scheduling, device clocks and desktop activity may differ. The few trials give no confidence interval. Quick checks have one trial per path; the 3 MiB reciprocal check has ten epochs and two trials. Cache-hit and traffic counters belong to separate profiling evidence. Logical bytes/time does not measure physical DRAM bandwidth or a universal texture advantage.
