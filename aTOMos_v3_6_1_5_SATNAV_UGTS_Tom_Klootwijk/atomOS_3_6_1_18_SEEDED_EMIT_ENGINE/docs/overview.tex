\section*{Exact words and complete native artifact streams}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Exact words and complete native artifact streams}
R18 extends the native operator engine with an optional complete ordered
EMIT journal. Artifacts compiled by the original seeded workflow can now
be materialized from native GPU execution, including every successful
emission inside fused chunks. Canonical Cell48, State64, faults, receipts
and ASA/NA/JK words retain their existing meanings.

The stream extends the finite transition proof: equal transitions produce
the same event predicate, record and next state. Disjoint lane intervals
preserve order independently of GPU scheduling. Proven capacity is
allocated before mutable execution; source identity and complete boundaries
govern continuation. Prefix and fault outcomes remain explicit. A compact
EMIT journal is distinct from a full instruction trace or final receipt.

Two original seeded fixtures reproduce 3,461 and 131,517 artifact bytes
under native execution. Fresh original Python replay verifies every
ordered event field and final State64 word. The compiler still evaluates
formal expressions on the host before encoding their result as EMIT cells.
Native materialization does not yet move that evaluator or learner search
onto the GPU.

PHI/TIME, KLEIN and LSYS phase expressions also gain exact bounded forms,
with the signed-wide reference retained. Independent checks cover complete
quotients, remainders, seams, signed extrema and feedback. Matched phase
measurements report both gains and regressions; their final-only device
costs do not measure complete artifact throughput. Orbital comparisons
separately match physical inputs, frames and horizons; exact words alone
supply no satellite-position accuracy ranking.

\subsection*{Preserved lineage and continuing scope}
The complete R17 and earlier chapters follow the new R18 material. Earlier
reports retain their original release, input, hardware and observation
scope. Statements describing a then-missing feature remain historical;
the R18 chapters identify what has since been implemented and checked.

The imported PHI remains a finite phase counter, distinct from exact
$a+b\varphi$ arithmetic; SDF0 still assigns zero. Lossless one-bit-plane
transport preserves complete operator words. Broader native expression
lowering, non-affine event discovery and dynamic geometry/cache rebinding
remain continuing work. Physical applications require their declared
models, observations and error criteria. Source provenance, final-byte
document review and package integrity provide separate evidence.
