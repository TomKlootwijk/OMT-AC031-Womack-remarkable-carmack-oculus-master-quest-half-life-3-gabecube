\section{R18: exact phase and seam arithmetic in 32 bits}
\label{sec:r18-phase}

The original WQK GPU sources contain a useful way to avoid wide signed
intermediates while preserving the finite machine. R18 implements the
bounded identities below in the shared transition. The old wide expressions
remain selectable through \code{ATOMOS\_WIDE\_PHASE\_REFERENCE} for matched
comparison. The canonical C interpreter is unchanged. Full bounds and source
relationships are given in \path{formal/EXACT_PHASE32.md} and the retained
original-return audit.

\subsection{PHI and TIME retain the complete quotient}
For raw unsigned words $A,B$, let $a=\operatorname{s32}(A)$ and
$b=\operatorname{s32}(B)$. Take $M=2^p$, with $p=12$ for PHI and $p=14$
for TIME. The canonical Euclidean sum has $a+b=Mq+r$ and $0\le r<M$.
For each input define
\begin{equation}
 q_a=(A\gg p)-(A\gg31)2^{32-p},\qquad
 r_a=A\mathbin{\&}(M-1),
\end{equation}
and likewise for $B$. Quotient terms are converted to signed32 before
subtraction. The sign correction follows from
$\operatorname{s32}(A)=A-(A\gg31)2^{32}$, hence $a=Mq_a+r_a$.
Now compute
\begin{equation}
 t=r_a+r_b,\qquad q=q_a+q_b+(t\gg p),\qquad
 r=t\mathbin{\&}(M-1).
 \label{eq:r18-divmod}
\end{equation}
Substitution proves the required quotient and remainder. Each input
quotient lies in $[-2^{31-p},2^{31-p}-1]$, and $0\le t\le2M-2$.
The complete output quotient fits signed32: its PHI range is
$[-1048576,1048575]$ and its TIME range is $[-262144,262143]$.
Thus no signed-overflow assumption or wide sum is required.

The complete $q$ remains observable. PHI tests $q\ne0$ for wrap status and
uses its parity for the selected branch/orientation behavior. TIME mixes
the raw word of $q$ into lineage. Keeping only parity or remainder would
change execution. The canonical old key, cell identity, lineage postlude
and successor rule remain intact.

\subsection{KLEIN and LSYS use exact modular phase updates}
For raw rho word $R$, the finite KLEIN update uses
\begin{equation}
 \rho'=R\mathbin{\&}(2^{20}-1),\qquad
 \epsilon=(R\gg20)\mathbin{\&}1.
\end{equation}
Signed interpretation changes the quotient by $2^{12}$ when the sign bit
is set. That change is even, so remainder and seam parity are preserved.
For an odd seam, set $\theta_0=\Theta\mathbin{\&}(2^{18}-1)$. The source
half-turn or reflection respectively yields
\begin{equation}
 \theta'=(\theta_0+2^{17})\bmod2^{18},\qquad
 \theta'=(2^{17}-\theta_0)\bmod2^{18},\qquad
 \phi'=(-\Phi)\bmod2^{12}.
\end{equation}
Unsigned wrapping followed by masking is exact because these periods divide
$2^{32}$. Preserve the source orientation, optional sheet, status, branch
and normalization. This keeps the finite parity; it adds no winding history.

For LSYS, let $o=\mathrm{orientation}\mathbin{\&}1$ and
$b=\mathrm{branch}\mathbin{\&}1$. With raw turn argument $A$, the canonical
chirality/branch product is positive exactly when $o\oplus b=1$. Thus
\begin{equation}
 D=\begin{cases}A&o\oplus b=1,\\-A\pmod{2^{32}}&o\oplus b=0,\end{cases}
 \qquad\Phi'=(\Phi+D)\mathbin{\&}(2^{12}-1).
\end{equation}
This equals the signed-wide phase expression even for $-2^{31}$ arguments
and raw, non-normalized phases. Rate division remains R17's separately
proved truncation-toward-zero magnitude shift. PHI/TIME floor division
and LSYS rate truncation are distinct contracts.

Equal subexpressions preserve the canonical transition and its ordered
fused composition. Boundary checks, compiled resources and matched timings
provide separate evidence. Fewer source operations alone establish no
speedup, cache-hit rate or physical accuracy.
