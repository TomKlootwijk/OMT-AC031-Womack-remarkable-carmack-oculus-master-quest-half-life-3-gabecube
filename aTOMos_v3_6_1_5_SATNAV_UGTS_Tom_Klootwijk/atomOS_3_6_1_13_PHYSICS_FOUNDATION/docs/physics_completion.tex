\subsection{A specialization now has an explicit completion criterion}
For a chosen application, completion of the mathematical profile means that
the state and inputs are declared, equations and domains close, relevant
boundary exchanges are accounted for, the finite evolution is named, and the
requested output has a defined meaning. A derived invariant must cite the
particular model and update for which it holds. A physical tolerance additionally
needs justified input and model bounds for that application.

This is a useful architecture for specialization because a new kernel can
reuse exact arithmetic, words, delayed state, units and source bindings while
importing only the required physical equations. The worked finite mechanical
profile demonstrates that separation: its rational solve and energy identity
need no general electromagnetic solver. An optical-material specialization,
in contrast, needs the selected electromagnetic and thermal closure.

\subsection{Implementation obligations remain narrow and visible}
No new XOP runtime, automatic compiler, proof checker, material calibration or
physical executor is claimed. The discrete midpoint equations provide a
complete mathematical step on their domain, but a program must still implement
the exact field solve, state commit and output contract. The new material models
remain conditional on their parameters, initial/boundary data and chosen finite
representation. Their physical applicability needs corresponding evidence.

The profile does not replace arbitrary continuum fields by a finite state
without a declared reduction. It does not equate energy balance with correct
trajectory, make a physical clock exact by encoding its ticks, or restore
information lost before acquisition. It also changes no earlier satellite
accuracy result and adds no general quantum or gravitational theory.
