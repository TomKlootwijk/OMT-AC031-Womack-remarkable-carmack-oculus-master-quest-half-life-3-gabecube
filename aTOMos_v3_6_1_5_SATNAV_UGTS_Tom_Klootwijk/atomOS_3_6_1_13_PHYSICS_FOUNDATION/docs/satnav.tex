\section*{Scope, lineage and method}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Scope, lineage and method}
This supplement addresses a specific requirement: strengthen the foundation
with actual physics and only the additions that help its formalization.
It adds a minimal law-profile contract, finite mechanical power balance,
thermodynamic and electromagnetic closure, and measurement/output conditions.
It does not repeat the complete 145-page R12 corpus or introduce unrelated
application families.

The parent is \code{atomOS\_3\_6\_1\_12\_PHOTONIC\_INTERFACE}, committed at
\code{96a2192}. Its complete PDF and source identities are bound in
\code{source/PARENT\_BINDING.json}. Its XOP word/operator contracts, WANTWOMBAN
equations, ASA/NA and JK transitions, orbital models and receiver profiles
remain the authority for those definitions. R13 uses named templates over
that vocabulary; no existing profile is silently replaced.

Established physical laws retain their external scientific provenance.
Constitutive examples state the selected material assumptions. The new
integration decisions and finite-step proofs are derived in this document.
Mathematical consistency, faithful implementation and physical adequacy are
different claims with different evidence.

Following the continuing work method, no reference programs, numerical
examples, algorithm tests, simulations, benchmarks or physical experiments
were executed. The work consists of equation derivation, primary-source
inspection, critical review and document preparation. The proofs are readable
conditional arguments, not proof-assistant certificates. Rendering and file
hashes verify delivery and provenance, not the proposed system's performance.
