\paragraph{Metric and information counterexamples.}
For source page 5, let
$\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$, $H_4=H_2\otimes H_2$ and
$A=\operatorname{diag}(\phi,\phi^{-1},1,-1)H_4$. Then
\[
H_4^T H_4=I,\qquad |\det A|=1,\qquad
\|A(1,0,0,0)^T\|_2^2
=\frac{\phi^2+\phi^{-2}+2}{4}=\frac54.
\]
Thus $A$ preserves four-dimensional volume magnitude, not Euclidean lengths
or all areas. Page 9's integer implementation also maps both
$(0,0,0,0)$ and $(-1024,-1024,0,0)$ to zero after its masks: the second input
has sums $(-2048,0,-2048,0)$. Both inputs fit the declared fields. This
arithmetic cannot serve as a lossless physical-state encoding.

\paragraph{Executable corrections.}
Page 8's conditional mask is always zero because a right shift by 63 yields
only 0 or 1, while its mask has bit zero clear. Page 9's bit complement
represents $-x-1$, not $-x$. Page 12's addition of 4 is not a bit-2 toggle:
$4+4=8$, whereas XOR toggles exactly the desired bit. On page 14, a half-turn
in the 11-bit angle field $[51:41]$ requires XOR of word bit 51; the printed
mask flips ten bits. Finally, declaring left-to-right notation does not alter
C/C++ precedence. For page 16's expression, log-radius 2 and occupancy 0
produce 2 under C rules but 0 under strict left-to-right evaluation. Explicit
parentheses or a specified parser are necessary.
\par\end{samepage}

\paragraph{Ground-station direction and chart limits.}
The points $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ have equal radius and can have equal occupancy,
yet their bearings differ by $\pi/2$. The source's radius/occupancy OTAN2
therefore cannot replace geometric azimuth; it is also distinct from the
existing UGTS OTAN2 profile. Keep the physical relative position, ENU axes,
range, and defined cases for azimuth/elevation.
An 11-bit full-turn angle has a half-bin rounding error of
$0.087890625^\circ$. At an illustrative $R=42{,}164{,}000$ m,
\[
e_{\rm chord}=2R\sin(\pi/4096)=64{,}678.76\ \mathrm m.
\]
Even a single-angle 10 m quantization budget at this radius requires at least
24 angular bits; this allocates nothing to other error sources. A station
calculation uses actual slant range. Log radius requires
$\rho=\log_2(r/r_\star)$ with $r,r_\star>0$; the origin has no finite value.
The page-4 log of a trigonometric product is undefined at zeros and unbounded
below, so it cannot establish page 7's assumed nonnegative monotonic motion.
A parent-local interval $[1,2]$ misses the slit $x=0$, while its child translated
by $-1$ intersects it. Culling needs an envelope of every descendant.
Similarly, $\det\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,-1)=+1$: two axis flips do not
establish non-orientability.

\paragraph{Exact time and derived keys.}
For integer ticks $n$, reference tick $n_0$ and positive integer period $P$,
\[
n-n_0=qP+r,\qquad q=\left\lfloor\frac{n-n_0}{P}\right\rfloor,
\qquad 0\le r<P.
\]
Keeping both $q$ and $r$ preserves time exactly, including negative elapsed
ticks; phase alone aliases times separated by a period. Orbital GPST/UTC/TT/TDB
conversion and the full epoch remain authoritative. The calendar and constants
in the filename derive no orbital initial condition or force constant.
Log-polar values, occupancy and phase can supply derived display addresses or
keys with declared origin, domain, quantization and winding. They do not replace
metre-valued geometry or establish cryptographic unpredictability.

\paragraph{The accepted R10 packing construction.}
For a $64\times64$ bit array, the transpose is
\[
B'_{c,r}=B_{r,c},\qquad T^2=I.
\]
Six stages exchange corresponding row-index and column-index bits. Each stage
is a permutation, so no numeric information is lost. R10 uses this exact
transpose for its existing bitplane format, retaining binary64 bit patterns,
types, ordering, padding and canonical digest. This is an engineering
construction inspired by the source's binary-word organization; the source
does not itself specify or prove this algorithm. It imposes no Hadamard
arithmetic or golden-ratio quantization on physical coordinates. Release
round-trip checks and measured benchmarks establish correctness and speed;
this source audit makes no timing claim. Representational exactness does not
remove the independently measured physical-model discrepancy.
