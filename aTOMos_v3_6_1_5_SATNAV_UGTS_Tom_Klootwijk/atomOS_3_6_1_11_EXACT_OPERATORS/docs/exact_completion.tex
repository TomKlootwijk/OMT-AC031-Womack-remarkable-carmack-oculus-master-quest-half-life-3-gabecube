\section{Logical coverage and implementation boundary}
\label{sec:exact-completion}
The new chapters supply the expression types and bit-word representation, causal
state semantics, exact arithmetic constructions, original attitude recurrences,
and explicit lifts of the retained physical and spatial equations. The companion
registry in this volume supplies the complete literal/type/reference grammar,
ordered byte fields and assigned operator IDs. The companion
\path{formal/COVERAGE_AND_PROOFS.md} maps requirements F01--F14 to their
definitions and conditional arguments. \path{formal/STRATEGY_REVISIONS.md}
records the proposed changes, deductions and resulting strategy choices.

\subsection{What the deductions establish}
For a finite, well-formed seed, the declared grammar and inverse bit transpose
preserve its exact record. Given defined operators, admitted inputs and valid
rewrites, each retained state root denotes the exact selected recurrence.
Positive-root normalization gives its stated invariant; homogeneous quaternion
readout removes roots only where the identity applies. A shared graph can avoid
duplicating a construction while retaining all its mathematical dependencies.

These conclusions have visible premises. The review compares definitions,
source equations, signs, domains, timing and proof steps. It is human-readable
mathematical reasoning, not execution evidence or a proof-assistant certificate.
The release contains no new algorithm test, numerical simulation, benchmark or
physical-accuracy experiment.

\subsection{Corrections made by reasoning}
The accepted strategy preserves source variants that initially appear similar:
the original Madgwick report's magnetic-reference timing and its appendix code
are distinct; finite-gain mixing and the simplified correction have different
increments; an ambient quaternion gradient and the gradient through normalization
are different derivatives. These differences determine the recurrence itself.

Likewise, nine fitted orbital-frame entries need not form an exact orthogonal
matrix. Exact RK4 does not acquire the inverse/group property of a continuous
flow. The native and Python receiver implementations have different range-domain
policies. The formalization names these choices instead of merging them in an
equivalence claim. Exact literal imports, lazy branch demand, root signs, input
chronology and the winding discarded by modular keys remain explicit.

The format review also made frame and clock identities part of the canonical
record, completed algebraic literal and component-projection fields, and made
mixed-dimension state types explicit. A coefficient jump uses the continuous
integral-solution definition. CGK's declared failure tuple preserves its new
word and old mechanical state atomically, while finite Jacobi diagnostics remain
separate from an ideal eigenvalue problem. These are recorded in revisions
S01--S26 and the logical-review record.

The adopted improvements are therefore structural: retain exact operators,
reuse identical bound expressions, cancel roots under proved premises, separate
readout from state, and record semantic changes as new profiles. There is no
claim of a measured execution-speed gain from this reasoning alone.

\subsection{What is still implementation work}
A future executable engine needs a conforming parser, exact integer/rational
arithmetic, algebraic representations and decision procedures, scoped graph
storage, a sound proof checker, and certified output evaluation. CPU and GPU
execution must implement the same semantics and report resource exhaustion
explicitly. Neither a finite graph nor one-bit word storage proves that a live
workload fits a device's memory or timing budget.

Unrestricted transcendental and implicit-solution decisions do not have a
promised general terminating oracle. Retaining a term, an unresolved decision
or an unmet domain obligation is a specified outcome. Such an outcome is not a
finite numerical prediction. A continuous ODE declaration needs justified
existence and continuation, and is not an implemented exact orbit solver.

Physical conclusions additionally require supplied measurements, calibrated
frames and clocks, an applicable force/sensor model, and a stated application
tolerance. Exact internal arithmetic does not turn uncertain coefficients into
physical truth or remove time discretization. The dated R10 measurements that
follow remain evidence for their original numerical implementation and sample
scope. They do not validate the newly specified exact expression engine.
