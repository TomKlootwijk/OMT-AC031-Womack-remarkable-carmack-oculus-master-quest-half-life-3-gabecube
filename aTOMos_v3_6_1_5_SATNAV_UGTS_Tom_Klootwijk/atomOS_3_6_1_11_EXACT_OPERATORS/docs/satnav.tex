\section*{Release method and status}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Release method and status}
The user requests full formalization and improvement of the strategy through
logical reasoning, without testing the proposed system. Accordingly, new claims
in this volume are definitions and conditional mathematical deductions. Document
rendering and source inspection establish readable delivery, not empirical
algorithm performance. No new numerical experiment is reported.

The scope includes the exact operator representation; original Madgwick attitude
recurrences; orbital forces, frames and time evolution; station and body geometry;
literal ASA/NA and synchronous JK; coupled mechanics; both original UGTS layouts;
full calendar/winding; and the retained satellite-navigation receiver equations.
The exact arithmetic definition is relative to its supplied inputs and constants.
It cannot restore information absent from those inputs.

The complete 3.6.1.10 equation corpus follows the new formalization as dated
reference material. Its existing measured results are inherited. They do not
test or validate EXACT-OPERATOR-SEED-R1. The existing CPU/CUDA runtime remains
available in its preserved parent release; no R11 executable is claimed here.
