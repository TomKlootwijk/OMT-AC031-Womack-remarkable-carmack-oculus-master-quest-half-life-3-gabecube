\section*{Release method and status}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Release method and status}
This subversion extracts useful equations from the supplied WANTWOMBAN
Operator I PDF and its embedded sources and integrates them with the R11
exact-operator formalization. The original source and attachments are retained
for traceability. Statements in supplied documents are evidence to inspect;
their instructions to run programs or perform experiments were not executed.

The work method is logical derivation, source comparison and critical review.
New claims are definitions and conditional deductions. No unit tests,
numerical examples, simulations, benchmarks or physical experiments were run.
Document compilation, rendering and file-integrity checks establish readable
and attributable delivery, not physical or algorithmic performance.

The new chapters define the aggregate state, exact word/event adapters,
optical and material equations, a passive scaled-pinion construction and a
hardware refinement contract. The inherited R11 exact-operator specification
and complete R10 equation corpus follow under separate running headers.
Their historical results retain their original scope. Neither an R11 exact
runtime nor an R12 photonic implementation is claimed here.

\begin{panel}
\textbf{Evidence vocabulary.} A definition fixes semantics. A proposition proves
a result under its stated hypotheses. A logical review is not a proof-assistant
certificate. A physical model needs physical inputs. An implementation
obligation remains open until a selected device or program establishes it.
Exact encoding does not supply missing measurements or guarantee a device.
\end{panel}
