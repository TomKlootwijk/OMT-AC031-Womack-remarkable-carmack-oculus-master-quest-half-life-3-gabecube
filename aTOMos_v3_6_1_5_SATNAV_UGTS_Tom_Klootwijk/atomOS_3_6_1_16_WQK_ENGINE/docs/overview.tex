The word machine's arithmetic is deliberately finite: operands, statuses,
flags and modulo operations have literal bit semantics. Its source
\code{PHI} is a 12-bit phase and its \code{SDF0} is a zero assignment.
Those names do not extend the meaning of the retained exact golden-ratio
algebra or metric signed-distance fields. The new binding is separately
versioned, so the unmodified VM remains independently testable.

The event profile works from an equation, rather than from a time grid.
For an admitted affine segment and plane, the root is a rational quotient.
The engine checks every remaining relation, certifies the complete earliest
group and publishes one successor epoch. A returned root therefore does not
depend on a sampling interval or an epsilon displacement. This exactness is
relative to the supplied rational model and its fixed frame.

The native upload transposes complete words into one-bit planes and
reconstructs the canonical program on the GPU. Integer texture fetches
preserve those bits; the persistent state feeds later instructions. CUDA
controls execution through its runtime and driver. Cache replacement and
memory bandwidth remain hardware behavior to measure.
