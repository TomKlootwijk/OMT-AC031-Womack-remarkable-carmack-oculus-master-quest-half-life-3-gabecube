\section{Specialization and remaining engine scope}
The engine now executes literal finite operator programs on the GPU and
closes their emitted-word feedback loop. The exact event adapter also derives
affine crossing times, applies simultaneous state/rate changes and replays
their mathematical certificates. These additions support deterministic
procedural machines, discrete control logic, exact piecewise-affine events,
reproducible state transport and the retained batched spherical-query profiles.

The imported learner is studied and source-pinned, but its authored finite
candidate families and publication machinery are not incorporated into the
runtime. A finite exact-consistency search does not establish noisy calibration
or statistical confidence. The release adds the operator and event mechanisms
that are directly executable here.

The retained Maxwell, mechanical, thermal, gravity and measurement equations
support physical specializations with supplied parameters, boundary conditions
and observations. They do not determine an unknown satellite state, material
response or optical transfer function. Application accuracy must be measured
against that application's declared output and tolerance.

The next engine work is broader native expression lowering, exact or certified
non-affine root isolation, versioned dynamic geometry refit and cache rebinding,
and measured operation coverage beyond the existing spherical radius/count
profiles. The R16 event adapter is a CPU rational implementation with once-only
relations. Its immutable epoch transition is not yet a GPU BVH refit. The
TOMAGI word machine and the inherited $a+b\varphi$ evaluator retain distinct
instruction and overflow contracts.

The editable master is \path{docs/unified.tex}. The \path{formal/} directory
specifies executable domains; \path{review/} distinguishes R16 validation from
historical R15 measurements. Source hashes bind preserved releases, the imported
WQK authorities and the immutable clock record. The main-repository PDF is
byte-identical to the visually reviewed release PDF.
