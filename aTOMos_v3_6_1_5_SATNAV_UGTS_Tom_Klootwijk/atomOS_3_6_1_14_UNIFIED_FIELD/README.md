# aTOMos 3.6.1.14 - unified field formalization

The current consolidated foundation combines literal ASA/NA and JK feedback,
exact one-bit operator words, physical field laws, coupled energy and heat,
gravity/time geometry and calibrated observation in one PDF. It includes the
complete XOP-R1 wire/operator registry and a real NIST atomic-time service
observation latched as immutable source data.

The main repository also contains the identical PDF directly at its root:
`aTOMos_v3_6_1_14_Unified_Field_Formalization_Tom_Klootwijk.pdf`.
The editable master is `docs/unified.tex`; this release's PDF is in `output/pdf/`.

## Concrete additions

- One common electromagnetic/polarization/mechanical/entropy energy generates
  reciprocal force, constitutive temperature and loss destinations, with derived
  total energy and entropy balances.
- `FIELD-MIDPOINT-QUADRATIC-R1` defines a finite exact field/heat step with a
  unique solve and discrete work, heat and entropy identities under its fixed
  passive quadratic assumptions. R13's mechanical step remains included.
- A shared gravity potential, full frame acceleration, proper-time convention
  and two-event signal model connect orbit and station observations.
- `ATOMOS-ATOMIC-TIME-ANCHOR-R1` retains the actual NIST request/reply and exact
  timestamp, a dated IANA leap table, nominal TAI/GPST/TT/TCG mappings, and explicit
  one-time latch/domain bindings. It does not set the host clock or establish
  a continuously disciplined local atomic oscillator.
- Continuous fields and geometry remain mathematical expressions. A raster is
  not required; selecting a finite reduction or time update is explicit.

`formal/` contains the operator catalog and integration contracts. `source/`
contains provenance, inherited source reference records and the clock capture.
`review/` separates logical review, requested acquisition and document inspection.
Older releases are preserved and remain the authority for historical runtimes,
transport profiles and measured results when those optional profiles are selected.

This is a unified mathematical field/state framework, not a claim of new
fundamental force unification. No new exact XOP runtime, compiler, proof checker,
photonic hardware or satellite accuracy is claimed. Work consists of logical
derivation, primary-source review and document inspection, plus the user's
explicitly requested real internet clock acquisition. No proposed kernels,
reference programs, numerical trials, tests, simulations or benchmarks ran.

Build: `python tools/build_pdf.py --engine PATH_TO_TECTONIC`.
Document inspection: `python tools/review_pdf.py --render`.
Release packaging and root-PDF publication: `python tools/package_release.py`.
These tools prepare document artifacts and provenance, not the proposed kernels.
