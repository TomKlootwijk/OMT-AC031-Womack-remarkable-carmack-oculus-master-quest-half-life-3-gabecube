\section{Specialization, proof scope and delivered authority}
\label{sec:r14-completion}
The unified foundation now contains actual common bodies, rather than only
shared names: literal word feedback, exact operator storage, continuous
physical balances, a coupled energy function, finite work/heat updates,
gravity/time geometry and an acquisition/uncertainty contract. The latched
NIST observation supplies one real time reference whose identity can be shared
by those domains. It supplies no missing initial field, material coefficient,
station coordinate or detector calibration.

\subsection{The minimal specialization record}
An application supplies the record $\Pi$ defined in
Section~\ref{sec:r13-core}, its actual equation bodies, and the following
bindings. Each dependency is included only when the selected outputs use it.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}P{30mm}Y@{}}
\toprule Binding & Required content\\\midrule
State & Types, frames, initial values or acquired initial-state requirements,
and the complete exact roots retained after a step\\
Time & Source event and epoch, scale map, acquisition and availability intervals,
clock uncertainty and the logical step policy\\
Physics & Applicable laws, constitutive energy/response, geometry, independent
boundary data, sources and admitted domain\\
Coupling & Shared energy, reciprocal derivatives, power ports, heat destinations,
and parameter-switch work\\
Evolution & Continuous solution declaration or specified finite step,
existence/domain obligations and its own invariants or defects\\
Observation & Actual transduction and projection, unit/clock/frame realization,
correlated uncertainty, requested output and tolerance\\
Realization & Selected software or physical encoding, resource bounds and
the evidence supporting the claims actually made\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}\end{center}

An omitted unused domain requires no surrogate equation. An omitted required
input remains an open dependency. This makes the formalized core reusable
without adding a catalogue of unrelated laws. Conservation, well-posedness,
physical correspondence and accuracy are distinct obligations, each tied to
the equations and observations to which it applies.

\subsection{What the equations establish}
The literal bit-plane transform is invertible on complete canonical seeds.
The delayed typed graph has a unique next state whenever its demanded
operations are defined. The common continuous field/thermal model has the
derived energy and entropy balances on its admitted domain. The mechanical
and quadratic-field midpoint profiles have their own exact discrete work
identities and unique solves under their fixed passive assumptions. The
gravity and acquisition equations tie clocks, coordinates and physical
outputs together. The measurement bound gives a sufficient tolerance
condition when the actual parameters and model/output errors meet its premises.

These results are mathematical consequences within the specified models.
The source observations support their recorded values and provenance. No
new executable XOP engine, proof assistant, manufactured photonic arithmetic,
general physical performance or improved satellite accuracy is claimed.
The logical reviews are internal independent reviews; physical correspondence
requires the selected application's observations and characterization.

\subsection{One current document in the main repository}
The main-repository PDF and the release PDF have the same bytes. The editable
master is \code{docs/unified.tex}. The word/operator catalog is included in
the appendix; the corresponding canonical source is
\code{formal/OPERATOR\_CATALOG.md}. Domain derivations and exact clock binding
are in \code{formal/}. Raw NIST time data and the IANA leap table are in

The review record separates mathematical review, the requested clock
observation, visual PDF inspection and artifact byte integrity. The release
manifest and archive preserve the delivered sources. Older releases remain
available with their original profiles, definitions and historical evidence.
