\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{One formalization, connected by explicit equations}
This release integrates the exact seed, literal state feedback, physical field
models and measurement contract in one document. Its contribution is their
explicit mathematical composition. Maxwell's equations, classical mechanics,
thermodynamics and weak-field gravity retain their established scientific
provenance. Material response, finite reductions and instrument parameters
are selected models whose applicability must be established for a device.

The central construction uses a common total energy for finite electromagnetic,
polarization, mechanical and thermal states. Reciprocal interactions follow
from its derivatives. Dissipation transfers energy into the declared thermal
stores exactly once. A separate finite quadratic-field step derives its own
discrete work and heat identity. The gravity/time profile connects acceleration,
clock rate, source time and ground-station observation under one convention.
These mathematical bodies become typed XOP expressions inside the same causal
ASA/NA and JK architecture; a separate display does not overwrite exact roots.

spatial and calibration specializations. It is not an automatic selection of
the right physics for every application. Each specialization closes its own
inputs, boundaries, admissible domain and requested output. A finite continuum

\subsection*{Continuous fields remain equations}
The authoritative field may be a function of continuous space and time.
For example, a slit in a declared plane has the literal predicate
\[
 A(x)=\boldsymbol1\{|a\cdot(x-x_c)|\le w/2\}
      \boldsymbol1\{|b\cdot(x-x_c)|\le h/2\},
 \quad a\cdot b=0,\quad\|a\|=\|b\|=1,
\]
with exact parameter expressions and explicit physical uncertainty. Its
definition has no pixels. A function, integral or ODE solution can likewise
retain exact denotation without rasterizing its domain. The XOP catalog gives
the corresponding domain conditions; a retained solution term is not a claim
that a finite executor has evaluated every real value. Selecting a modal
truncation or finite time step is an explicit application choice. A display
sampling never becomes the authoritative field merely because it was rendered.
