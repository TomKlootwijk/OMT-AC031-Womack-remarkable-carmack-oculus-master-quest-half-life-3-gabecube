## Material corrections incorporated

1. The clock reference is the actual stratum-1 NIST response, including its
   version-3 server header, although the request used version 4. No metadata
   was silently promoted to authentication or receiver UTC accuracy.
2. A dated IANA leap table supplies the captured ordinary-second conversion;
   TAI/GPST/TT/TCG mappings retain exact rational operands and explicit epochs.
3. The latch uses lazy admission and freezes optional edge memory, so a
   completed anchor requires no future network record, pulse or edge input.
4. The schedule explicitly selects the event-adapted synchronous JK word
   before command selection; both base J/K still read the same old state.
5. Photon-count addition across frequency components requires incoherence,
   orthogonal resolution or beat averaging; a general coherent finite-window
   measurement retains the required cross terms.
6. A finite endpoint update does not by itself define an acquisition integral;
   its trajectory or reconstruction must be supplied.
7. Dissipation deposited in a thermal store excludes radiation/exported power
   unless the chosen model actually thermalizes it there.
8. Relative tidal acceleration, geocentric clock potential, frame acceleration
   and signal propagation share stated conventions without double counting.
9. The word-valued expressions have Bits(w) type; that does not misclassify
   integer popcounts or other non-word results.
10. The common nonlinear energy proof remains distinct from the fixed
    quadratic finite-step proof. No arbitrary-step trajectory accuracy follows.
