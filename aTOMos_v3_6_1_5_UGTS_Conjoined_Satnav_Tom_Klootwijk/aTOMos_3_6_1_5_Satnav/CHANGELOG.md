# aTOMos 3.6.1.5 — conjoined satnav profile SN1

Combines the retained aTOMos 3.6.1 word/JK/OTAN2 contracts with UGTS-KC
3.6.2 SCLP geometry, local-chart calculus and distinct 64-bit codecs.
Adds the explicitly named prepared-code positioning model SN1, a tested CPU
implementation, CUDA global/FP64 texture source, CMake builds, fixtures and
independent review tools.

The new GNSS measurement equation and numerical QR solver are SN1 additions,
not formulas claimed to have appeared in the supplied UGTS document.
CUDA compile and execution remain not_run in this delivery environment.
